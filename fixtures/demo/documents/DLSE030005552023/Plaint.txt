SUIT FOR PERMANENT AND MANDATORY INJUNCTION

1. That the plaintiff is the owner in possession of the ground floor of property no. B-214, Sheikh Sarai Phase-I, New Delhi, by virtue of a registered sale deed.

2. That the defendant, who resides on the first floor, has raised an unauthorised construction on the common staircase and has blocked the plaintiff's access to the terrace.
3. That the plaintiff served a legal notice on the defendant, which was received but not replied to.

PRAYER: (a) a decree of permanent injunction restraining the defendant from raising any further construction on the common staircase; (b) a decree of mandatory injunction directing the defendant to remove the construction already raised.
